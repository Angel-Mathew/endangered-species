# Endangered Species Dashboard


A dashboard about 65 endangered species, showing what type of animal or plant each one is, how many are left and what threatens them.

## The problem
Conservation work depends on knowing which species are in the most danger and why. I wanted to put species type, population and threats in one place.

## What you can explore
- A species picker by type (Amphibian, Bird, Crustacean, Fish, Fungi, Insect, Mammal, Plant)
- Population by species
- A treemap of species count by animal type
- A table of species, type, population and threats

## What I found
- Mammals are the biggest group (17 species), then birds (10), amphibians (8), reptiles (7), and plants and fish (6 each).
- The total population across the species is 7,738, but it is very uneven. The Northern bald ibis has about 3.0K, while the Belin vetchling has just 1.
- Threats include habitat degradation, urbanisation, agriculture, fire, disease and recreational facilities.
